The University of Oklahoma has announced a record 6,437 freshmen for fall 2026, the largest first-year class in both state and university history and the sixth consecutive year of record freshman enrollment, according to OU.

## The Class of 2030 by the Numbers

OU says incoming class sizes are up 45% over the six-year streak, and Oklahoma freshman enrollment has now set a record four years in a row. About 28% of the class is first-generation, a university record; among Oklahoma freshmen the share is about 37%.

Academically, the class averages a 3.63 unweighted GPA and a 3.83 weighted GPA. The university counted 702 students with a 4.0 and 299 who ranked first in their high school class. A total of 917 students qualify for Oklahoma's Promise, up 19% from last year, and 137 are Oklahoma State Regents' Scholars, up 17%.

## Affordability and Housing

OU says average annual net tuition and fees for resident undergraduates have fallen 20% over seven years while need-based aid has risen 39%, and that 58% of undergraduates graduate debt-free, compared with 45% nationally. A new residence hall, South Hall, opened this fall with about 580 beds for first-year students.

President Joseph Harroz Jr. said "there has never been a more exciting time to be a Sooner." Jeff Blahnik, vice president for enrollment management, called the class "a powerful signal of OU's momentum."
