Oklahoma State University opened the fall 2026 semester with 37,411 students across its five-campus system, and the university reports records in several key categories, according to figures released by OSU in late August.

## Records Across Several Categories

OSU counted a record 23,233 full-time students and an all-time high of 3,289 online students. Enrollment of Native American students also reached a record 3,657. Oklahoma resident enrollment stands at 17,999, which the university says is the highest since 1984.

The incoming freshman class is the third-largest in OSU history. The university's own news release puts the freshman count at 4,714, while another OSU-sourced report lists 4,712, a small discrepancy that likely reflects when the numbers were pulled.

## A Strong Year for Oklahoma Campuses

OSU's numbers arrived alongside strong reports from other Oklahoma institutions. The University of Oklahoma announced a record freshman class of 6,437 students the same week, extending a growth streak that has made enrollment a defining story for the state's flagship universities.

For Stillwater, the in-state figure is the most notable number. Resident enrollment at its highest level in more than four decades suggests that more Oklahoma high school graduates are choosing to stay in the state for college, a trend state higher-education leaders have said they want to encourage.

The growth in online students, meanwhile, points to demand from learners who cannot relocate to a campus, including working adults and rural residents.
