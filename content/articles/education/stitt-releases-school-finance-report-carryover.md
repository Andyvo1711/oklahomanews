Gov. Kevin Stitt on Aug. 12 released a report on Oklahoma school finance prepared by Dan Hamlin during his time as Secretary of Education, under Executive Order 2026-12.

## Key Findings

The report found school district carryover balances grew from $2.8 billion to $4.5 billion between 2019 and 2025. An Oklahoma Council of Public Affairs analysis estimates that equals roughly 30% of annual public school revenue in 2025.

Districts earned $121.5 million in interest in 2025 and $115.4 million in 2024, which the report notes was a return of less than 3% in both years. It also highlights substantial variation in the number of in-person instructional days districts offer, and discrepancies between calendars published on district websites and figures reported to the State Department of Education.

## Policy Options

Among the ideas the report raises are changes to how carryover funds and interest earnings are handled, exempting districts from excess-carryover penalties, bond-funded endowments, and property tax revenue for charter schools.

## An Annual Review

The governor's office says the report is intended to be annual, establishing an ongoing review of selected parts of the school finance system by the Office of the Secretary of Education.
