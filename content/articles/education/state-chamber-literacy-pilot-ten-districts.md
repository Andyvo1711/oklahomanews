The Oklahoma State Chamber Research Foundation on Aug. 4 launched the Oklahoma Literacy Initiative, a privately funded $1.5 million pilot aimed at improving early reading instruction.

## How the Pilot Works

The program gives participating districts literacy leaders and regional consultants and offers educators professional learning and classroom coaching grounded in the Science of Reading.

Participating districts, as listed in coverage, are Beaver, Burns Flat-Dill City, Oklahoma City's W.K. Jackson Leadership Academy, Tulsa, Okmulgee, Meeker, McAlester, Maysville, Ardmore and Valliant, a mix of urban, midsize and rural systems. One news brief lists a different count, so exact participation may vary by source.

## Alongside State Efforts

The pilot is separate from the state's own work. Gov. Stitt signed Senate Bill 1778 on April 21, expanding the Strong Readers Act; teacher training funded under it includes an early-literacy micro-credential and a $3,000 state-funded stipend.

Chamber leaders have tied literacy to the state's education ranking and long-term workforce prospects, and the pilot is designed to show what sustained coaching can accomplish before any wider rollout.
