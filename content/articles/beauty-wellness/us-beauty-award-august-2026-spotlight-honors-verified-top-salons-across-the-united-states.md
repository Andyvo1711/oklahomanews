*The US Beauty Award announces seven Verified TOP Salon recipients for August 2026, recognizing beauty establishments across six states for their commitment to quality, craftsmanship and client care.*

## **Virginia: Experience and Client Trust**

### **Escape Nails & Spa, Midlothian**

Led by Ms. Nga Pham, Escape Nails & Spa places service quality, safety and customer satisfaction at the center of its approach. The salon's commitment to listening to client feedback and continually improving the experience has earned it a place among the August 2026 Verified TOP Salon recipients.

### **The NAIL Place, Virginia Beach**

With Ms. Trinh Nguyen bringing 24 years of salon ownership experience, The NAIL Place has been shaped by consistent service and a philosophy of treating clients like family. Its recognition reflects a career built on long-term client relationships and dedication to the beauty profession.

## **Maryland and Connecticut: Care in Every Detail**

### **Pink Nail & Spa, Glen Burnie, Maryland**

Drawing from a family background in the beauty industry in Vietnam, Mr. Y Nhu Tran has built Pink Nail & Spa around cleanliness, attention to detail and customer satisfaction, values that continue to guide the salon as it grows.

### **Thulan Nail Retreat, West Hartford, Connecticut**

Mrs. Thu Lan has shaped Thulan Nail Retreat as a welcoming space where meticulous nail care meets relaxation. Careful technique, sanitation and attentive service form the foundation of the salon's approach to its clients.

## **Florida: Growing Through Dedication**

### **Luxury Nails and Hair, Pembroke Pines**

Founded by Mr. Linh La and his wife, Luxury Nails and Hair has grown over the past five years through service refinement, staff development and a commitment to quality. The salon's recognition marks an important milestone in the couple's shared beauty journey.

## **Arizona: A Decade of Commitment**

### **Star Nails, Laveen Village**

For Ms. Linda Nguyen, ten years of building Star Nails have been shaped by adaptability and a willingness to listen. Client feedback and continued attention to changing beauty trends remain important parts of the salon's approach.

## **Texas: Personalized Beauty Care**

### **Castle Nails & Spa, Abilene**

With more than two decades in business, Ms. Cindy Nguyen has built Castle Nails & Spa around craftsmanship and highly personalized client care. Following its Verified TOP Salon recognition, the business is preparing for further growth with plans for an additional location.

## **Seven Salons and Seven Distinctive Journeys**

The August 2026 recipients represent different generations of beauty professionals, from emerging businesses to salons backed by decades of experience. Across six states, each has developed its own approach to craftsmanship, service and building lasting relationships with clients.

Through its monthly Verified TOP Salon recognitions, the US Beauty Award continues to highlight beauty establishments that meet its evaluation standards while encouraging professionals to continually strengthen service quality and the overall client experience.
