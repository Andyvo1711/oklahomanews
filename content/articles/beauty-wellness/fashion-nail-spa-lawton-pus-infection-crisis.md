A pedicure is intended to be a relaxing experience, but according to one customer review involving **Fashion Nail & Spa in Lawton**, the appointment instead became an experience the client described as painful, stressful, and ultimately alarming because of complications that reportedly followed the service.

## A Tense Experience From the Beginning

The reviewer described the technician as impatient throughout the appointment, saying her feet were repeatedly pulled or repositioned abruptly whenever she did not move quickly enough.

Rather than feeling comfortable, the customer said she felt rushed and increasingly anxious during what should have been a relaxing treatment.

## Multiple Cuts During the Pedicure

According to the review, the technician accidentally clipped the surrounding skin several times while performing the service.

The customer also purchased a paraffin wax treatment expecting additional comfort. Instead, she reported that the warmth of the wax intensified the discomfort because of the fresh cuts sustained earlier in the appointment.

## Concerns After the Appointment

The reviewer stated that one toe later showed signs of infection, including drainage beneath the nail, and that she experienced enough discomfort to ask the technician to stop filing before the service had been completed.

The review reflects the customer's belief that the complications were connected to the injuries sustained during the appointment.

## Why Careful Technique Matters

Pedicure services require careful handling of both nails and surrounding skin. Even small accidental cuts can become significant concerns if clients experience prolonged pain or other complications afterward.

When customers report discomfort during a service, responding immediately and adjusting technique can help reduce the likelihood of further problems.

## Editorial Perspective

Customers often judge salons not only by the finished appearance of their nails but also by whether they feel safe and respected throughout the appointment.

The review involving Fashion Nail & Spa in Lawton illustrates how rough handling, accidental injuries, and concerns about post-service complications can overshadow every other aspect of the customer experience. Careful technique, respectful communication, and prompt attention to customer concerns remain essential to maintaining trust.
