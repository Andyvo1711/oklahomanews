Susan G. Komen's Oklahoma City MORE THAN PINK Walk was held Oct. 31 at Scissortail Park.

## Fundraising Goals

Komen pages listed a goal of $205,000, while a press release cited $250,000. Running totals shown on Komen pages differed, and no final figure was available.

## Other October Events

Elsewhere in Oklahoma, BCSOK held its POUND the pavement PINK 5K at Lake Stanley Draper on Oct. 3, and the Pawnee Nation held its breast cancer awareness walk on Oct. 22.

## Why It Matters

Early detection and support programs remain central to breast cancer outcomes, and community walks raise funds for screening, research and patient assistance.
