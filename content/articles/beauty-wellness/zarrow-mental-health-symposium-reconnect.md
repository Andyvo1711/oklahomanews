Mental Health Association Oklahoma held the Zarrow Mental Health Symposium, branded RE:CONNECT 2026, on Oct. 1-2 at the Arvest Convention Center in downtown Tulsa.

## Theme

The theme was "Recovery, Resilience, & Rebuilding Community." The symposium invited clinicians, community members, nonprofit workers and impacted individuals to attend in person.

## Who Spoke

Attendees heard from local and national experts and explored current science to better serve neighbors in Tulsa and beyond.

## Local Momentum

Tulsa has expanded mental health efforts, including a new mobile team funded by the City Council in late August.
