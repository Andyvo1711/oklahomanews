The Oklahoma Department of Mental Health and Substance Abuse Services launched a "You Good? 988" campaign on Aug. 27 ahead of September's Suicide Prevention Month.

## The Message

The campaign urges Oklahomans to regularly check on friends and family and to make sure people know help is available. Interim Commissioner Joshua Anderson said, "Suicide prevention starts long before a crisis."

## The Data

Between 2020 and 2024, men accounted for about 81% of suicide deaths in Oklahoma and people 60 and older 26%. The rural suicide rate was 28.3 per 100,000, compared with 20.2 in metropolitan areas. The campaign targets middle-aged men, veterans, older adults, rural Oklahomans and tribal communities.

## 988 Lifeline

The 988 Lifeline offers free, 24/7 mental health and substance use support by call or text. More information is at 988oklahoma.com.
