Rural public schools serving prekindergarten through 12th grade had until 11:59 p.m. Oct. 15 to apply for the Oklahoma State Department of Education's ROOTS grant, which funds physical education, fitness and wellness initiatives.

## Who Could Apply

The grant is aimed at rural districts, where facilities and staffing for physical education can be limited.

## Why It Matters

Supporters say stronger school fitness programs can improve student health and academic engagement, particularly in communities with few other recreation options.
