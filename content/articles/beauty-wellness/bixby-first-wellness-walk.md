Bixby held its first Wellness Walk on Saturday, Oct. 10, at Charley Young Park, a mental health awareness event aimed at reducing stigma.

## What Happened

The walk included community resources, family-friendly activities and opportunities to connect. Organizer and founder Darcy Linam said, "checking on someone and asking if they're okay can make a bigger difference than we realize."

## Looking Ahead

Organizers plan to bring the event back next year. No attendance figures were reported.

## Part of a Larger Push

The walk followed the state's "You Good? 988" campaign and the Zarrow Mental Health Symposium in downtown Tulsa on Oct. 1-2.
