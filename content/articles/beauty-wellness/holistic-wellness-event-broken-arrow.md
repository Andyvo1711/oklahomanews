The Holistic Wellness Event took place Oct. 3-4 at Stoney Creek Hotel & Conference Center in Broken Arrow, from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. each day.

## Vendors and Speakers

Organizers listed more than 100 vendors, including holistic health and wellness businesses and local food producers, plus food trucks. Saturday speakers included Leigh Ankrum on how trauma interferes with our lives; Sunday speakers included Andrea Reed, Cathy Meehan and Christie Fleetwood.

## Admission

Admission was free with registration, with donations welcomed. A VIP Friday reception was held Oct. 2.

## Sponsors

Sponsors included Erin Martin as wellness partner and 1820 Spa among bronze sponsors.
