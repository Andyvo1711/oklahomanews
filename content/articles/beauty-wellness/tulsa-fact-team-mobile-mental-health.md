The Tulsa City Council on Aug. 31 approved $441,084 in first-year funding for a new mobile mental health team, the Forensic Assertive Community Treatment, or FACT, team.

## Who It Serves

The team serves people with serious mental illness who repeatedly cycle through hospitals, emergency rooms, law enforcement and the justice system, especially those experiencing homelessness. Teams will go to people where they are, including shelters.

## Funding and Partners

The money comes from the Allergan opioid settlement. The program was developed with the Healthy Minds Policy Initiative and will be run by Family & Children's Services. It is part of a three-year model meant to show measurable results and attract future private funding.

## Council Comments

District 1 Councilor Vanessa Hall-Harper said, "This is a mobile unit. And so, it's a new approach," adding, "Sometimes people are taken to jail unnecessarily."
