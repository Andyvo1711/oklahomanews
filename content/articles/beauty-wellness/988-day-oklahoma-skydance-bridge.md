Gov. Kevin Stitt proclaimed Sept. 8 as 988 Day in Oklahoma, and the Skydance Bridge in Oklahoma City glowed purple and yellow that night.

## A Visible Reminder

The lighting was part of the state's Suicide Prevention Month activities, which also included a #StepUp4Hope initiative inviting people to create Walls of Hope and share resources.

## Funding Question

Lawmakers appropriated $5.9 million to keep 988 running for one more year, according to Healthy Minds Policy Initiative coverage, meaning funding will have to be revisited for 2027 and beyond.

## Everyday Check-Ins

The day echoed the state's "You Good?" campaign, which encourages simple, regular check-ins as a form of suicide prevention.
