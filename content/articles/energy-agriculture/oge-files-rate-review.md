OG&E on Oct. 1 filed a rate review with the Oklahoma Corporation Commission totaling $395 million, which the utility says could raise the average residential bill by $23.88.

## What It Funds

The request covers reliability projects completed since March 2024 across OG&E's 30,000-square-mile service area. The filing excludes increased electricity demand from data centers. OG&E reports 2025 system reliability of 99.6%. The utility said, "Strengthening the grid for customers is no longer possible at current rates set three years ago."

## Customer Programs

The proposal includes an $85-per-year senior discount for all customers 65 and older, a $13-per-month discount for more income-qualified customers, an arrearage management pilot, and a deposit-free prepay option.

## Horseshoe Lake

New generating units at Horseshoe Lake were finished early and $12 million under budget, OG&E said, and bringing them online early is expected to avoid $9 million to $16 million in winter 2026-27 capacity purchases.

## Timeline

Current rates stay in place during the review, which OG&E says will take about six months.
