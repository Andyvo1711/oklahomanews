Little Sahara Gen-Tie Holdings, an affiliate of Heelstone Renewable Energy, has asked the Oklahoma Corporation Commission for a certificate of authority to build an 11-mile transmission line tied to a solar and battery storage campus near Alva in Woods County, Oklahoma Energy Today reported Sept. 29.

## The Project

The case is PUD2026-000082. The line would carry up to 345 kilovolts on steel monopoles up to 150 feet tall with a right-of-way of 200 to 400 feet. Heelstone says land rights were acquired through voluntary private easements and lease agreements. Construction is planned for the third quarter of 2028, according to Senior Vice President of Development Jonathan Burke.

## Investment

Heelstone cites anticipated private investment of up to $1.2 billion in its Oklahoma portfolio and projected local tax payments in the hundreds of millions of dollars over the project's life. The article gave no solar capacity figure.

## Community Input

An open house was scheduled for Oct. 28 at Northwest Technology Center in Alva. Heelstone, based in Durham, North Carolina, is owned by Madrid-based Qualitas Energy.
