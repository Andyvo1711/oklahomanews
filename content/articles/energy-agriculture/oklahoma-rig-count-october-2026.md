Oklahoma's active rig count slipped by one in mid-October as the Arkoma Woodford fell to zero rigs, according to Baker Hughes data reported by Oklahoma Energy Today.

## Plays

In the Oct. 19 report, the Cana Woodford had 19 rigs, the Granite Wash 21, the Ardmore Woodford 2 and the Arkoma Woodford none, with no reported activity in the Mississippian. The two western Oklahoma plays have traded places for the top spot through October.

## Late October

Baker Hughes data as of Oct. 31 put the national total at 603 rigs with Oklahoma at 51; oil rigs nationally rose to 462 and gas rigs fell to 132.

## Production

EIA monthly data released Oct. 30 show Oklahoma crude output running below 2025 levels, roughly 2.4% lower for January through August by one tally. Meanwhile, state officials credited higher oil prices and drilling for support in September revenue.
