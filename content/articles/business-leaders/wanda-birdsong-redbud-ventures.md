Redbud Ventures, a diversified business enterprise operating across construction services, hospitality, and retail in eastern Oklahoma, has grown steadily under the leadership of chief executive Wanda Birdsong, who has guided the company's expansion from a single construction services contract into a multi-sector operation over the past several years.

Birdsong, who came to the company after a career in regional economic development consulting, has said her approach to growing the enterprise has emphasized diversification as a hedge against downturns in any single industry, a lesson she has attributed to watching other regional companies struggle when they remained overly dependent on a single client or sector.

## Diversifying Deliberately

Redbud Ventures' expansion into hospitality followed several years of steady growth in its original construction services business, with company leadership identifying an opportunity to serve travelers and event organizers in eastern Oklahoma's growing tourism corridor. Birdsong has said the decision to enter hospitality required building an entirely new set of operational expertise, distinct from the construction services that remained the company's core business.

The company's later expansion into retail followed a similar pattern, built around identifying underserved local markets rather than attempting to compete directly with larger regional or national retailers. Birdsong has described this approach as central to the company's overall strategy, seeking out gaps in the regional market rather than competing head-on in already crowded segments.

## Investing in Regional Workforce Development

As Redbud Ventures has grown across its various business lines, the company has placed emphasis on hiring and developing talent from within eastern Oklahoma communities, an approach Birdsong has said reflects the company's broader commitment to regional economic development rather than treating hiring as a purely transactional process.

The company has partnered with regional workforce development programs to identify candidates for its various business lines, from construction trades to hospitality management, building internal training pathways that allow employees to move between the company's different divisions as they develop new skills and interests.

Birdsong has said Redbud Ventures plans to continue evaluating additional business opportunities as they arise, guided by the same diversification philosophy that has shaped the company's growth so far, while maintaining its commitment to building regional employment opportunities across eastern Oklahoma communities.
