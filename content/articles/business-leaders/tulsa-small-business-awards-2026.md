The Tulsa Regional Chamber honored local entrepreneurs at its Tulsa Small Business Awards on Sept. 3.

## The Winners

David Chen of Chick-fil-A was named Small Business Person of the Year. Inspyral won Young Entrepreneur of the Year, Topeca Coffee won Entrepreneurial Success, and R.K. Black won Family Owned Business of the Year.

## Why Small Business Matters

The chamber's small business program is designed to spotlight owners who have grown employment and community involvement in the Tulsa region. The honorees span restaurants, technology, coffee roasting and a long-established family firm, a reflection of the region's varied economy.

## Statewide Recognition

Earlier in 2026, the U.S. Small Business Administration named Sand Springs manufacturer OGI Process Equipment its Oklahoma Manufacturer of the Year, another sign of recognition for Tulsa-area employers.
