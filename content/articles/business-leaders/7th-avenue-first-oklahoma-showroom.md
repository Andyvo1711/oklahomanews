Los Angeles-based modern furniture brand 7th Avenue announced Oct. 30 that it will open its first Oklahoma showroom in Oklahoma City, welcoming customers starting Nov. 6.

## The Showroom

The store is at 2124 Northwest Expressway in OAK, a 20-acre mixed-use district. The company, founded in 2022, says it has 28 stores nationwide and is known for its modular sofas, including a flagship model with water-repellent, machine-washable fabric. Visits are appointment-first, with walk-ins welcome.

## Leadership Comments

Founder and CEO Billy Shaw said, "As we continue to grow our retail footprint nationwide, expanding into Oklahoma is an exciting milestone." Co-founder and COO Josh Stinson pointed to neighbors such as Capital Grille, RH, Arhaus and Williams-Sonoma as reasons for choosing OAK.

## Retail Momentum

The opening adds to a string of national brands choosing the Oklahoma City market, where OAK has become a destination for upscale retail and dining.
