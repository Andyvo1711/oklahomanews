Chad Warmington, president and CEO of the State Chamber of Oklahoma, argued in an Oct. 11 opinion piece published by Oklahoma Business Voice that the state's job growth depends on federal permitting reform.

## The Argument

Warmington wrote that Oklahoma cannot compete for jobs if it cannot build the infrastructure those jobs require. Energy projects, transmission lines and industrial sites all face federal permitting steps that can stretch for years.

## A Common Theme

The message echoes priorities from other Oklahoma leaders. The chamber has also been active on workforce issues, including a literacy pilot launched in August and coverage of the state's new shorter bachelor's degrees.

The State Chamber's State of Business Forum is scheduled for Nov. 17.
