OG&E's board of directors on Oct. 5 named John Laws chief operating officer of the utility, effective Jan. 4, 2027, according to a filing by parent OGE Energy Corp.

## From State Government to the Utility

Laws, 52, has been OG&E's chief commercial officer since January 2026. He previously served as Oklahoma's Secretary of Budget and state chief financial officer from October 2022 to December 2024, and was executive vice president, chief financial officer and treasurer of Enable Midstream Partners from January 2016 to December 2021.

## Timing

The filing did not name a predecessor or say whether the role was previously vacant. Compensation will be set during the company's annual officer compensation review in December.

## A Busy Month for the Utility

The announcement came days before OG&E filed its first rate review with the Oklahoma Corporation Commission since 2023, a $395 million request tied to reliability projects.
