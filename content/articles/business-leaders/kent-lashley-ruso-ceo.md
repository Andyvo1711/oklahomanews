The Regional University System of Oklahoma named Kent Lashley its chief executive, starting Sept. 1, Oklahoma Voice reported Aug. 21.

## A Finance Background

Lashley has served as executive vice president and chief financial officer at Rose State College. He will be the system's third CEO appointed since June 2024, a turnover he acknowledged himself.

## The Role

As CEO, Lashley will serve as liaison between the system's governing board and its member universities. RUSO governs several of the state's regional public universities.

## Stability Ahead

Leadership churn at the top of the system has drawn attention from lawmakers and campus communities, who will look to Lashley for steady management of budgets and enrollment at regional campuses.
