Expand Energy's board named Daniel E. Brown, 51, president and chief executive officer, effective Oct. 26, according to a regulatory filing reported by Oklahoma Energy Today.

## A Veteran Shale Executive

Brown succeeds Michael A. Wichterich, who served as interim CEO after Domenic J. Dell'Osso Jr. departed when the company moved its headquarters from Oklahoma City to Houston. Wichterich returns to his role as non-executive board chair.

Brown was president and CEO of Chord Energy from July 2024 to October 2026 and a board member from 2021 to 2026. He ran Oasis Petroleum, Chord's predecessor, from April 2021 to July 2024. Earlier he held senior roles at Anadarko Petroleum and Kerr-McGee from 1998 until Occidental Petroleum acquired Anadarko, including executive vice president of U.S. Onshore Operations.

## Compensation

The filing lists a $1 million base salary, a cash bonus target of 125% of salary and an annual long-term incentive target of $8.75 million. A one-time make-whole award is meant to replace pay he forfeited at his prior employer.

## Oklahoma Ties

Expand, formed from the merger of Chesapeake Energy and Southwestern Energy, long anchored Oklahoma City's business community, and its headquarters move was a notable loss for the metro.
