Oklahoma City Mayor David Holt said Sept. 29 that the city is in "very active conversations" with Buc-ee's about its first Oklahoma location, proposed for Interstate 40 and Cimarron Road in west Oklahoma City.

## Council Action

The City Council approved an agreement involving the Oklahoma Department of Transportation and a development company tied to Buc-ee's to build new infrastructure for the project. Holt did not announce a construction or opening date. "Though work lies ahead, we look forward to the opportunity to welcome Buc-ee's to OKC," he said.

## The Founder's View

Co-founder Arch "Beaver" Aplin III, speaking Aug. 17 at the opening of the chain's first Arkansas store in Benton, said, "when you find a conservative business-friendly state with a phenomenal workforce, it makes a difference."

## What Is Not Confirmed

Buc-ee's had not confirmed the location or timeline, and Oklahoma City does not yet appear on its list of upcoming openings, which names 2027 sites in Louisiana, Kansas, Tennessee, Florida, Texas and Georgia. The company says opening dates may change.
