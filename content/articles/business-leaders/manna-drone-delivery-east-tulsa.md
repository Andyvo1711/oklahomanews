Manna Air Delivery has expanded in Tulsa with a new operational base in east Tulsa, the Tulsa Flyer reported Oct. 19.

## The Expansion

Abbie de Vera, the company's community engagement manager, stood at the new base on Oct. 15. She said Manna chose east Tulsa because it is rich in culture and in different cuisines.

## Testing Demand

The move is part of an effort to learn whether Tulsa residents will adopt drone delivery for food and small items. The Tulsa Flyer's coverage framed it as a test of the city's appetite for the service.

## What to Watch

Drone delivery raises questions about noise, airspace and neighborhood impact that local officials and residents will weigh as the service grows.
