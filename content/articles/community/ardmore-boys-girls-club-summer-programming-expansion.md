The Boys and Girls Club in Ardmore has expanded its summer programming capacity this year, adding staff and activity space to reduce a waiting list that organization leaders say had grown steadily over recent summers as demand for affordable childcare options increased.

Club administrators describe summer as historically the organization's highest-demand season, since many working families in the Ardmore area lack access to affordable full-day childcare once school lets out, making the club's low-cost summer program one of relatively few options for parents who can't afford private summer camps or don't have family members available to watch children during the workday.

## A Growing Waiting List Prompts Expansion

Club leaders say the waiting list for summer enrollment had become long enough in recent years that some families were being turned away entirely, a situation that pushed the organization to prioritize capacity expansion as its top fundraising and planning goal. The expansion includes additional staff hired and trained ahead of the summer season, along with renovated space that allows the club to serve more children at once without exceeding safe staff-to-child ratios.

Funding for the expansion came through a combination of local fundraising, grant support, and increased operating budget allocated specifically to summer programming, according to club administrators, who describe the investment as directly tied to demand data showing how many families were being placed on waiting lists in prior years.

## Serving Working Families Across Southern Oklahoma

Club leaders emphasize that the expanded capacity serves a specific and persistent community need: working parents, particularly those in hourly or shift-based jobs without flexible schedules, who have few affordable options for full-day childcare once the school year ends. The club's programming includes structured activities, meals, and academic enrichment designed to keep children engaged throughout the day rather than functioning purely as basic childcare supervision.

Organization leaders say they will monitor enrollment and waiting list data again ahead of next summer to determine whether further expansion is needed, describing the current increase in capacity as a meaningful step but not necessarily a complete solution to the level of demand the club continues to see from families across the Ardmore area and surrounding southern Oklahoma communities.
