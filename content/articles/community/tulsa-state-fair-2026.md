The Tulsa State Fair is scheduled for Oct. 1-11, 2026, at Expo Square, continuing a tradition that draws visitors from across northeastern Oklahoma.

## Attendance

Fair officials reported that about 100,000 fewer people attended in 2025 than in 2024, but around 1 million still came. A 2023 figure listed on Wikipedia was 1,075,000.

## The Venue

Expo Square on East 21st Street hosts the fair's rides, exhibits, livestock shows and concerts. Listings differ on some details of the 2026 schedule, so visitors were advised to check the fair's website.

## Eleven Days of Activity

The fair also coincided with Tulsa-area wellness events, including the Zarrow Mental Health Symposium on Oct. 1-2.
