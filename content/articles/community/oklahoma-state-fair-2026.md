The 2026 Oklahoma State Fair ran Sept. 17-27 in Oklahoma City, according to visitor listings.

## A Fall Tradition

The fair is one of the state's largest annual gatherings, combining livestock competitions, carnival rides, concerts and food.

## Agriculture Backdrop

This year's fair took place as drought gripped the state. U.S. Drought Monitor data showed 100% of Oklahoma was in dryness or drought by early September, with almost 89% in severe, extreme or exceptional drought, making the livestock and crop exhibits a reminder of the pressures facing producers.

## Other Fall Events

The Plaza District Festival and Oklahoma City Oktoberfest were among other large Oklahoma City events in late September.
