The Regional Food Bank of Oklahoma held its signature fundraiser, Hope's Harvest Luncheon, on Oct. 21.

## Fighting Hunger

The Regional Food Bank serves central and western Oklahoma through partner agencies and distributions. The luncheon supports its work as demand for food assistance remains high.

## Volunteers Needed

The food bank runs volunteer shifts Tuesday through Saturday for individuals and groups of all sizes. In eastern Oklahoma, the Food Bank of Eastern Oklahoma said in an Oct. 28 Oklahoma Magazine piece that in 2025 more than 12,000 volunteers donated nearly 60,000 hours.

## Holiday Season Ahead

Food banks traditionally see surging demand heading into Thanksgiving and the winter holidays, and often ask for donations and volunteers during the fall.
