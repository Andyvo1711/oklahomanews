Choctaw Nation Chief Gary Batton delivered the annual State of the Nation address on Sept. 4 from the Capitol Amphitheater in Tvshkahomma, kicking off Labor Day festivities.

## Council Action

At its Sept. 12 regular session, the Choctaw Nation Tribal Council approved the Nation's fiscal year 2027 annual tribal budget and passed a lead-based paint code.

## Language Revitalization

The Nation also launched a free language-learning app, Chahta Anumpuli, part of its work to teach and preserve the Choctaw language.

## Why It Matters

With one of the largest tribal governments in the state, the Choctaw Nation's budget and programs affect health care, education and jobs across southeastern Oklahoma.
