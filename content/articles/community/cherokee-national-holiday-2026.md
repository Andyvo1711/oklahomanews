The Cherokee Nation held its 74th Cherokee National Holiday Sept. 4-6 in Tahlequah, the tribe's capital.

## A Constitution Commemorated

The holiday marks the signing of the Cherokee Nation's 1839 constitution and is one of the largest annual gatherings in northeastern Oklahoma, drawing citizens from across the country.

## A Milestone Year for the Nation

The celebration came weeks after the Cherokee Nation opened its 127-bed W.W. Hastings Hospital in Tahlequah, a $470 million facility connected to the tribe's outpatient health center, and a $30 million wellness center that replaced the Male Seminary Recreation Center.

## Tribes Gather Across the State

Other nations hosted Labor Day gatherings, including the Choctaw Nation's State of the Nation address on Sept. 4 and the Chickasaw Nation's annual meeting and festival later in the month. Together the events underscore how tribal governments are central to community life in much of Oklahoma.
