The 27th annual Plaza District Festival was held Sept. 26 in Oklahoma City's Plaza District, running from noon to 11 p.m., according to a weekend event roundup.

## A Neighborhood Showcase

The festival highlights the arts-oriented district's shops, restaurants and performance spaces.

## A Busy Weekend

That weekend's events also included ZooBrew at the Oklahoma City Zoo, a Down Syndrome Festival and 5K at Myriad Gardens and Mesta Festa in Perle Mesta Park. Earlier in September, Scissortail Park hosted Ballet Under the Stars on Sept. 11.

## Community Identity

Street festivals like these are a main way Oklahoma City neighborhoods build identity and bring in visitors.
