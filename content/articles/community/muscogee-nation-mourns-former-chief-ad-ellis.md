The Muscogee Nation announced Sept. 17 the death of former Principal Chief A.D. Ellis at age 90.

## A Life of Service

Ellis served as principal chief of the Muscogee Nation, one of the Five Tribes, whose capital is in Okmulgee.

## Other Tribal Losses

The Peoria Tribe of Indians of Oklahoma announced Sept. 1 that former Chief John Froman had died at age 65. Both losses were noted in a tribal roundup published by NonDoc in early October.

## Tribal Meetings Continue

The Muscogee Nation also hosted the Inter-tribal Council of the Five Civilized Tribes for a quarterly meeting Oct. 7-9 in Durant.
