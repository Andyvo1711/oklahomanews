The Chickasaw Nation's 2026 Annual Meeting and Festival ran Sept. 25 through Oct. 3 in Tishomingo, marking the 66th Chickasaw Annual Meeting and 38th Chickasaw Festival.

## A Gathering of Citizens

The annual event brings Chickasaw citizens to the tribe's historic capital for cultural activities, tribal business and community celebration.

## A Year of Growth

The festival follows a significant investment summer for the tribe, which broke ground July 31 on the Chickasaw Nation Health Newcastle Medical Center after its Legislature approved a $1 billion first-phase development budget.

## Community Traditions

Annual meetings like this one anchor the calendar for many families in south-central Oklahoma and help keep traditions of language, dance and storytelling alive.
