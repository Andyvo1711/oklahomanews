Oklahoma's seasonally adjusted unemployment rate fell 0.1 percentage point to 4.2% in September, the Oklahoma Employment Security Commission reported Oct. 20.

## Matching the Nation

The U.S. rate rose 0.1 point to 4.2% in September, so the two rates are now equal. Oklahoma's rate is 0.6 point higher than in September 2025.

## Other Details

Based on commission-based summaries, roughly 84,200 Oklahomans were unemployed, about 1,000 fewer than in August, while payroll jobs rose by only about 100, suggesting limited employment growth. Love County had the highest county rate at 6.1% and Dewey County the lowest at 2.1%. The unadjusted rate was 3.9%.

## What Comes Next

The next federal state-level release is scheduled for Nov. 20. State Treasurer Todd Russ's September report noted August's Oklahoma rate at 4.3% and cautioned that inflation and fuel costs warrant monitoring.
