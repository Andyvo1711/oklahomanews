Manufacturing activity in the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City's Tenth District, which includes Oklahoma, continued to increase in September, according to the bank's monthly survey covered by Oklahoma Energy Today.

## Survey Findings

Respondents said activity kept rising and expectations for future activity remained steady. The survey covers Oklahoma through the bank's Oklahoma City branch. A later October survey described activity as continuing at a solid pace, with firms expecting increases in the near term.

## State Backdrop

State Treasurer Todd Russ's September report showed a Business Conditions Index of 55.4, above the growth-neutral 50, with manufactured goods exports up 16.3%.

## Tariff Concerns

Oklahoma Business Voice reported Sept. 10 that one Oklahoma manufacturer said tariffs were raising its export costs and complicating relationships with Canadian customers, a reminder that trade policy remains a risk for exporters.
