Consultants reviewing Oklahoma's economic development programs recommended preserving several incentives that help businesses buy equipment, expand operations and compete for investment, Oklahoma Business Voice reported Oct. 12.

## Recommendations

The review suggested keeping the Quick Action Closing Fund while expanding public reporting on its results. It also called for changes to some tax breaks.

## Context

The state's Department of Commerce reported 19 announcements of new or expanding companies in the second quarter, with anticipated investment of $2.07 billion, and said a record 329 applications came in to its Oklahoma Innovation Expansion Program for 2026, with 262 companies approved to create 1,211 jobs.

## Why It Matters

Lawmakers use periodic incentive reviews to decide which programs to renew. The results are likely to inform debate over how the state competes for large projects such as the proposed Inola aluminum smelter.
