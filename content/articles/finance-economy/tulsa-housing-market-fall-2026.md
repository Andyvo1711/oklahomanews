Oklahoma's two largest housing markets are moving at different speeds this fall, based on figures from national data trackers rather than local Realtor associations.

## Tulsa

Redfin reported Tulsa's median sale price at about $260,000 for the three months ending in September, up 4.0% from a year earlier, with homes selling after 24 days on the market versus 26 days last year. Realtor.com described Tulsa as a seller's market in August, with a median listing price of $274,950 and median sale price of $273,000.

## Oklahoma City

Realtor.com described Oklahoma City as balanced in August, with a median listing price of $268,400 and a median sale price of $270,000. Zillow's typical home value data for the second quarter showed Tulsa up 2.8% year over year and Oklahoma City down 0.3%.

## Caveat

Figures vary by source and method, and readers should check metro Realtor associations' monthly reports for official numbers. The Treasurer's report noted the average 30-year mortgage rate reached 7.03% in late September, a backdrop for affordability.
