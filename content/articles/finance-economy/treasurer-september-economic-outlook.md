Oklahoma tax receipts grew in all five major categories in September compared with a year earlier, according to State Treasurer Todd Russ's September Economic Outlook Report.

## Revenue Growth

Gross production tax was $95.4 million, up 6.3%. Income tax rose $101.1 million, or 15.5%, and sales and use tax rose $71.4 million, or 12.8%. Motor vehicle tax was up 1.3% and other taxes up 2.3%.

## Business Conditions

The Business Conditions Index was 55.4, above the growth-neutral level of 50, with production, new orders and employment all expanding. Manufactured goods exports rose 16.3%.

## Cautions

Russ said, "Oklahoma's revenue performance continues to show growth across major tax sources," while noting that fuel costs and inflation warrant monitoring. The report cited CPI up 0.6% in September for annual inflation of 3.7%, and an average 30-year mortgage rate of 7.03% in the final week of September. Higher oil prices and drilling supported the energy sector, while rising gasoline prices pressured households.
