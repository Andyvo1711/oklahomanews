August General Revenue Fund collections totaled $604.4 million, $7.8 million, or 1.3%, below the monthly estimate and $5.3 million, or 0.9%, above August 2025, state officials reported Sept. 15.

## Category Results

Income tax collections were $230.0 million, 7.0% below estimate and 5.2% below a year earlier. Gross production tax was $22.7 million, 37.8% below estimate and 19.0% below last year. Sales tax came in at $205.8 million, slightly above estimate and up 4.5% year over year, and use tax was $48.2 million, up 9.5% from August 2025.

## Year to Date

Fiscal year-to-date collections stand at $1.1 billion, $15.6 million, or 1.4%, below estimate and $87.5 million, or 7.2%, below the same period of FY 2026.

## Officials' View

OMES Director Mark Wood said collections "remain generally in line with expectations for the early months of the new fiscal year," attributing some shortfalls to timing and corporate income tax variability and calling the broader picture steady.
