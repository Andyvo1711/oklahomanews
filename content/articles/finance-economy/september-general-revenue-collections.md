Oklahoma's General Revenue Fund took in $924.2 million in September, which is $170.3 million, or 22.6%, above the monthly estimate and $154.7 million, or 20.1%, above September 2025, the Office of Management and Enterprise Services reported.

## By Category

Total income tax collections were $552.1 million, up 30.9% over estimate and 21.0% from a year ago. Gross production tax was $40.5 million, up 3.7% over estimate and 28.7% from last year. Sales tax came in at $202.0 million, up 1.7% over estimate and 8.7% year over year. Use tax was $53.1 million, up 19.9% over estimate.

## Year to Date

For the first three months of fiscal year 2027, collections total about $2 billion, $154.7 million, or 8.2%, above estimate and $67.2 million, or 3.4%, above the same period last year.

## Timing Factors

OMES Director Mark Wood said September's results "reflect some of the timing factors we anticipated earlier in the fiscal year."
