Newman Memorial Hospital in Shattuck, in northwest Oklahoma, is working to restore labor and delivery services, a rare move at a time when rural maternity units have been closing across the country, according to an October StateImpact Oklahoma report.

## A Second for Oklahoma

Once the unit opens, Newman Memorial is expected to be Oklahoma's second critical access hospital to deliver babies. Certified nurse midwife Amy Calhoun-Vasko will handle low-risk births.

## Rural Health Money

Oklahoma received more than $223 million from the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services for the first year of the federal Rural Health Transformation Program. A state Department of Health project funds a certified nurse midwife and expands access through new clinics in Shattuck and Woodward. Reporting did not specify how much of the funding goes to the hospital's unit itself.

## Risks Ahead

CEO Tom Vasko said the hospital will also have to contend with federal Medicaid cuts that will affect Oklahoma's directed payment program. The hospital's plan illustrates the balancing act facing rural providers: federal investment aimed at transforming rural care alongside reductions that threaten the finances of the facilities expected to deliver it.
