The Chickasaw Nation broke ground July 31 on Chickasaw Nation Health Newcastle Medical Center, which the tribe says will be one of the largest First American hospitals in the country, according to reporting published in early August.

## The Investment

The Chickasaw Nation Legislature approved a $1 billion development budget for phase one in June. The first phase is planned with 50 inpatient beds, an emergency department, surgical suites, labor and delivery, family medicine, pediatrics, specialty clinics, behavioral health, dental clinics and a full pharmacy.

## Location and Timeline

The medical center is planned for Newcastle, south of Oklahoma City in McClain County. No confirmed opening date was reported.

## Tribal Health Investment

The project follows the Cherokee Nation's opening of its $470 million W.W. Hastings Hospital in Tahlequah in July, signaling a wave of tribal investment in health infrastructure at a time when federal funding changes are straining other providers across the state.
