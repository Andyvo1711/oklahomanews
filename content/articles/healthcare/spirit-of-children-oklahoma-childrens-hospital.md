Oklahoma Children's Hospital OU Health is again a beneficiary of Spirit Halloween's Spirit of Children program, which held its annual fundraising week Oct. 12-16.

## A Long Partnership

A News 9 interview with the hospital said the partnership began in 2010 and described October as a favorite month. Customers can round up purchases at Spirit Halloween stores or donate through the Spirit of Children page on the company's website.

## The National Campaign

Spirit Halloween's release listed 167 pediatric hospitals participating nationwide, with a goal of $20 million. The company says 100% of in-store and online donations stay local to the hospital community where they are raised.

## Easing Hospital Stays

Local coverage described the effort as part of work to make hospital stays less frightening for children, through events and activities that bring some normalcy to seasonal celebrations. No Oklahoma-specific fundraising total had been released at the time of reporting.
