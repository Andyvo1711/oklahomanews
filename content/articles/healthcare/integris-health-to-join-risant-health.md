INTEGRIS Health, Oklahoma's largest nonprofit health system, announced Oct. 21 that it has agreed to join Risant Health, the nonprofit subsidiary of Kaiser Permanente, pending regulatory approval.

## The Deal

INTEGRIS would become the third health system in Risant's portfolio, joining Geisinger and Cone Health. The Oklahoma system operates 10 acute and 10 specialty hospitals along with physician clinics and urgent care locations, and employs about 13,000 people.

INTEGRIS says it will keep its name and brand, along with its medical staff and leadership team. Risant's interim CEO expects the transaction to close around the end of the first quarter of 2027. Financial terms were not detailed in initial announcements.

## Financial Backdrop

The announcement follows months of financial pressure. In April, INTEGRIS announced reductions in physician numbers and the closure of some services after projecting a $130 million annual net revenue loss tied to changes in Medicaid and Medicare funding.

## What Patients Can Expect

Both organizations have described the arrangement as a way to preserve local control while gaining access to national resources. Regulatory review will determine the timeline, and day-to-day care at INTEGRIS hospitals and clinics is not expected to change in the interim.
