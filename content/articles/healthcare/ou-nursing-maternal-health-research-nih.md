The University of Oklahoma College of Nursing will support four maternal health research projects over the next four years with more than $5 million from a National Institutes of Health initiative, OU announced Oct. 20.

## Building on CIRCLE

The work builds on CIRCLE, which was first funded in 2023 with $10.5 million to advance research on improving Indigenous maternal health in the Southern Plains region. One of the new projects, based in Alaska, is funded at $1,258,349 over four years.

## Why It Matters

Oklahoma has struggled with maternal health outcomes, and rural and tribal communities often face long distances to obstetric care. Research programs that partner with Indigenous communities aim to produce practices that fit local needs rather than importing models designed elsewhere.

## Part of a Larger Effort

The grants add to OU's growing health research portfolio. During his State of the University remarks in September, President Joseph Harroz Jr. said the university's Health Campus had entered the nation's top 100 for NIH funding for the first time.
