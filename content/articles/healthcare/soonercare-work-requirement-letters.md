The Oklahoma Health Care Authority began sending letters in September to SoonerCare members who may be affected by new federal work requirements that take effect Jan. 1, 2027.

## Who Is Affected

The requirements apply to adults ages 19 to 64 who are not otherwise exempt. Members will need to complete 80 hours per month of qualifying activity or demonstrate a minimum monthly income.

## Eligibility Renewal Changes

A Sept. 1 public notice proposed eligibility changes implementing parts of federal Public Law 119-21. Beginning Jan. 1, 2027, certain adults in the Medicaid adult expansion group would renew eligibility every six months instead of every 12, contingent on federal approval.

## Other Notices

The agency also posted notices that month on lactation consultant revisions and renewal of the ADvantage home- and community-based services waiver.

## What Members Should Do

OHCA says the letters are meant to give members time to prepare. Hospital leaders have warned that coverage losses could increase uncompensated care, a concern that has been central to financial pressures at hospitals across the state.
